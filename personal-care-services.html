Personal Care Services

Dignified, compassionate personal care

Your loved one keeps their privacy and routine intact — a trained caregiver simply makes the hardest parts of the day easier and safer.

Book a FREE local in-home consultation

Like all of our home care services, your personal care will be customized to meet your individual needs.

Bathing and grooming happen at your loved one’s own pace, in their own bathroom, with a caregiver trained to protect both dignity and safety.

Dressing assistance means the right clothes, done up properly, every morning — without the frustration or fall risk of struggling alone.

Mobility and transfer support helps your loved one out of bed, on and off the toilet, and in and out of the bath or shower — safely.

Toileting and continence care is handled with patience and discretion, so an awkward moment never becomes an isolating one.

Medication reminders keep the day’s doses on schedule, so you’re not the one calling at 9am to check.

Feeding assistance is there when it’s needed, from a steadying hand to full support at mealtimes.

Skin and pressure-area checks during care catch early redness or irritation before it becomes a bigger problem.
